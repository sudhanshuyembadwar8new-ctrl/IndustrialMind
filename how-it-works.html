Our Proven 5-Step Process

From first message to live website — here's exactly how it works.

Step 01

Discovery & Consultation

We start with a free WhatsApp consultation to understand your business, goals, target audience, and design preferences. We'll discuss your budget, timeline, and gather all the content you want on the site — text, images, contact details, and services. No commitment required at this stage.

⏱ 15-30 minutes • Free
Step 02

Proposal & Planning

Based on our discussion, we'll send you a clear proposal with the recommended plan, features list, exact pricing, and delivery timeline. You'll know exactly what you're getting — no hidden fees, no surprises. Once you confirm and make a 50% advance, we start building immediately.

⏱ 1-2 hours turnaround
Step 03

Design & Development

This is where the magic happens. We custom-design and hand-code your entire website from scratch — no templates, no WordPress themes. Every element is crafted to match your brand, with mobile-first responsiveness, smooth animations, WhatsApp integration, SEO setup, and conversion-optimized layouts. You'll receive progress updates via WhatsApp.

⏱ 1-7 days depending on plan
Step 04

Review & Revisions

We share a preview link for you to review on both desktop and mobile. Go through every section, test every button, and let us know what changes you'd like. We'll make all requested revisions quickly — usually within 24 hours. Your satisfaction is our top priority. We don't consider a project done until you're 100% happy.

⏱ 1-2 revision rounds (plan dependent)
Step 05

Launch & Go Live

Once approved, we deploy your website to a fast hosting platform (Netlify/Vercel) and connect your custom domain if you have one. You get the complete source code, a deployment guide, and post-launch support. Balance payment is due upon delivery. Optional maintenance plans available for ongoing support.

⏱ Same-day deployment

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay?

50% advance to start, 50% on delivery. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and other digital payment methods. No hidden processing fees.

What if I don't like the design?

We include revision rounds in every plan (1-3+ depending on your tier). We work closely with you to get the design exactly right before launch.

Do I need a domain?

Not required to start. We can deploy on a free subdomain first. If you want a custom domain (like yourbusiness.com), we can help you set it up.

Will I own the website?

Yes, 100%. You receive the complete source code and can host it anywhere. No vendor lock-in, no recurring fees for ownership.

What about hosting costs?

We deploy on platforms like Netlify or Vercel that offer generous free tiers. Most small-to-medium websites run completely free. No monthly hosting fees!

Can I update the website myself later?

The code is clean and well-structured. If you know basic HTML, you can make changes yourself. Otherwise, our maintenance plans cover all updates for you starting from ₹999/month.
