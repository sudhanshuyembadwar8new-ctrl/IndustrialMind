Our story

About Us

We're a friendly neighborhood store built on trust, care, and everyday service.

Since 2006, Sudhanshu-Manvi Supermart has been serving local families with groceries, essentials, and helpful services in a warm and reliable way. The shop is led by Mangesh Yembadwar, who believes in simple, honest service and treating every customer like a neighbor.

Whether you're stopping in for daily items or a quick service, you can count on a welcoming experience and dependable support every time.
